Convierte Odoo en tu fábrica de software Turn Odoo into your software factory

Core Odoo es una plataforma auto-hospedada que usa Odoo 20 como backbone —no como ERP— y te da un ciclo estilo Odoo.sh (dev → staging → prod) con frontends generados, agentes de IA y observabilidad completa en un solo host Docker. Core Odoo is a self-hosted platform that uses Odoo 20 as a backbone —not as an ERP— and gives you an Odoo.sh-style lifecycle (dev → staging → prod) with generated frontends, AI agents and full observability on a single Docker host.

01 · Qué es01 · What it is

Odoo como columna vertebral, no como ERP Odoo as a backbone, not as an ERP

No es un ERP nuevo ni un wrapper. Es la capa de ciclo de vida que convierte Odoo 20 en el backend de tus proyectos, sin reconstruir lo que Odoo ya resuelve. It is not a new ERP or a wrapper. It is the lifecycle layer that turns Odoo 20 into the backend of your projects, without rebuilding what Odoo already solves.
